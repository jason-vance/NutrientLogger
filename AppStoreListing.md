## Description
Nutrient Logger is the offline-first nutrition tracker for people who want to know what's actually in their food — not just calories and macros, but the full spectrum of vitamins, minerals, and amino acids your body needs.

Unlike cloud-based trackers, your food log never leaves your device. And unlike macro-focused apps, every entry breaks down 80+ nutrients so you can spot gaps before they become deficiencies.

Key Features:

* Detailed Nutrient Breakdown
See your daily intake of both macro and micronutrients, from protein and fiber to magnesium and vitamin B12.

* Extensive Food Database
Search from tens of thousands of foods, including whole ingredients and packaged items, to log meals with precision.

* Reusable Meals
Group foods you eat together often and save them as custom meals for faster, smarter logging.

* Personalized Nutrient Targets
View recommended daily values and upper intake limits based on your age and sex, helping you stay balanced and avoid deficiencies or excesses.

* Learn as You Log
Discover what each nutrient does for your body and why it matters to your overall health.

Nutrient Logger is perfect for anyone serious about nutrition—whether you’re on a specific diet, managing a deficiency, or just curious about your health. It’s time to go beyond calories and truly understand your food.
