## Main description (paste into the "tell us about it" field)

Nutrient Logger is a nutrition tracker built around two ideas most competitors ignore: **your health data should stay on your device**, and **nutrition is more than calories and macros**.

**Privacy by architecture, not policy.** The entire USDA food database — tens of thousands of foods — is bundled inside the app. Searching, logging, and analysis all happen on-device. There's no account, no cloud sync, no server that ever sees what a user eats. In a category where food logs are routinely uploaded and monetized, Nutrient Logger simply has nothing to upload. It works completely offline — on a plane, off the grid, anywhere.

**Depth mainstream trackers don't offer.** Every logged food is broken down into 80+ nutrients — vitamins, minerals, amino acids, and lipids — with personalized targets based on age and sex. Users don't just count calories; they discover the gaps in their diet before those gaps become deficiencies.

**What's new in 4.0** — the app's biggest release yet:
- An all-new Body tab: weight, body fat, BMI, and waist tracking with goal deadlines, pace projection, and a BMR/TDEE-based calorie target
- Weekly deficiency insights that flag the nutrients a user has been running low on — and suggest foods to close the gap
- A redesigned Nutrition tab with reorderable nutrients and trend charts for calories, macros, water, and every micronutrient
- Daily and weekly streaks with milestone achievements
- Apple Health sync (calories, macros, and water out; weight in), CSV export for sharing with a doctor or dietitian, and a redesigned onboarding flow

**Built natively for Apple platforms** by a solo developer: SwiftUI throughout, SwiftData for persistence, StoreKit 2 subscriptions, HealthKit integration, and a Home Screen widget.

Nutrient Logger is an indie answer to a real frustration: the leading micronutrient trackers interrupt logging with full-screen video ads and keep users' food history in the cloud. This app is the opposite — quiet, private, and focused on helping people genuinely understand what they eat.
